V9 reuses V8 bounded ZIP verification. Outputs only schema headers, known alias matches and aggregate counters; never actual prices, IDs, amounts or raw files. Quarantines duplicate/missing NEWT dissemination identifiers, NEWT with original ID, and NEWT directly referenced by same-day non-NEWT records. This is deliberately conservative, not a complete cross-date lifecycle reconstruction. Does not certify any active contract. Approximate 1750–1900 day effective-to-expiry filter is a research diagnostic, not a standard maturity test. Exact normalized issuer name is a provisional candidate, not a UPI/LEI mapping. Same-leg numeric spread and notation, currency, fixed rate and notional co-presence are separately counted; no independent counter establishes joint eligibility. A numeric value is not proof of a conventional spread. Notional rounding, payment sign, official model, authoritative entity and product mapping, lifecycle across days and exact contract dates remain unresolved.
